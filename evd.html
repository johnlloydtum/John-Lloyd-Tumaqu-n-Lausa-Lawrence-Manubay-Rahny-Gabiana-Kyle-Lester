Quick answers

FAQs

What services will GoPrint Kiosk offer?

The proposal includes printing, photocopying, scanning, basic document-formatting assistance, rush/same-day service when available, and a small selection of school supplies.

Can I place an order online?

The proposed business plans to accept pre-orders through a Facebook Page, Messenger, or a simple online order form. This website form is a demonstration and does not transmit a real order.

What paper sizes can I request?

The demo form lists A4, Letter, and Long/Legal as options. Confirm availability with the business before placing an actual order.

How much does color printing cost?

The feasibility study reports that most respondents currently pay ₱5–₱10 per page for color printing. GoPrint Kiosk's final prices have not yet been confirmed.

Do you offer rush or same-day printing?

Rush/same-day service is part of the proposed concept, subject to workload, file readiness, and confirmation by the kiosk.

Where is the kiosk located?

The study proposes a location near the campus, but an exact address has not been provided yet.

Can I upload my document here?

Not in this prototype. It only prepares a text summary of your preferences; file upload and actual order transmission are not connected.
